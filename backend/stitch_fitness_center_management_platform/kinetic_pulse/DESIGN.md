## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the high-octane, round-the-clock intensity of modern commercial fitness. Built for dynamic urban athletes, active lifestyle seekers, and fitness enthusiasts, the visual language balances urgent athletic energy with razor-sharp commercial efficiency. 

Drawing from **High-Contrast Bold** design, the system utilizes stark value contrast: saturated, hyper-energized red paired with deep obsidian charcoal on pure crisp white backdrops. The aesthetic communicates constant momentum, accessible round-the-clock power, and direct goal-oriented motivation. Photography is gritty, high-contrast, and action-oriented, while the UI interface itself remains pristinely clean, scannable, and friction-free to drive high conversion and effortless navigation.

## Colors

The palette leverages a sports-performance tri-color hierarchy:
- **Primary (`#E61B23`)**: High-voltage red reserved for top-priority callouts, action banners, primary conversion buttons, and active tabs. Never wash it out with weak gradients; use it flat and decisive.
- **Secondary (`#111116`)**: Deep carbon black for maximum typographic contrast, high-impact header navigation bars, and anchored CTA containers.
- **Tertiary (`#00A86B`)**: Athletic emerald green applied exclusively for confirmation badges, verified amenities, and live status cues.
- **Neutral (`#F7F7F9` and `#FFFFFF`)**: Pure clinical whites for surface containers layered over light cool-tinted grays for background section separation. Text levels drop from secondary carbon (`#111116`) to mid-slate (`#585862`) for supporting details.

## Typography

The typography pairing uses **Anybody** for bold athletic display headlines and pricing, juxtaposed with **Outfit** for crisp, geometric body copy and UI labels. 

- **Headlines & Numbers**: Display hero headings, promotional tags, and major pricing counters use Anybody in Extra-Bold and Black weights. Titles can utilize uppercase tracking or tight negative letter-spacing to simulate athletic jersey and gym branding.
- **Body & Controls**: Outfit provides geometric balance, exceptional screen legibility across all densities, and friendly, modern shapes that balance the aggression of the headline typeface.
- **Accents**: Important subheaders can feature oblique/italic posture alongside red directional slash accents (`//`) to introduce diagonal sports kinetics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an active 12-column responsive grid on desktop (max content width 1280px) and a dense 4-column grid on mobile devices.

- **Desktop**: 12-column grid with a `1.5rem` gutter and fluid `2rem` outer page margins. Card grids default to 3-column layouts for membership tiers and 2x3 or 3x2 grids for class/club exploration tiles.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 2-column stacked modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with tight `1rem` margins and gutters. Horizontal scroll carousels are preferred over multiline vertical card grids for membership cards and trainer profiles to maintain speed and thumb-friendly scan rates.
- **Section Rhythm**: Inter-section gaps rely on `space-xxl` (64px) to preserve stark breathing room against dense, bold visual elements.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy skeuomorphism or blurry layered shadows; depth is achieved primarily through **Tonal Stacking**, **Card Edge Definition**, and **Tactile Edge Transitions**.

- **Base Layer (Level 0)**: Canvas background `#F7F7F9` or full-bleed sports photography.
- **Card Tier (Level 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards with a crisp `1px solid rgba(17, 17, 22, 0.08)` outline and a very subtle ambient lift: `0px 4px 16px rgba(17, 17, 22, 0.04)`.
- **Active / Hover State (Level 2)**: Elevated membership and promotional cards transition cleanly to `0px 12px 28px rgba(230, 27, 35, 0.12)` with the top border or outline shifting to `#E61B23`.
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Navbars (Level 3)**: Fixed top navigation and mobile bottom quick-action triggers use solid `#E61B23` or deep `#111116` with a hard drop-shadow: `0px 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs an intentional contrast between **semi-rounded structured containers** and **full pill-shaped interactive controls**:

- **Containers & Cards**: Styled at `roundedness: 2` (8px inner radius, 12px-16px outer card container radius) for an architectural, robust framework.
- **Buttons, Badges & Floating CTAs**: Override container radii with full capsule/pill styling (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create high-contrast, touch-optimized visual tap targets.
- **Visual Slashes**: Diagonal sport slashes (angled at -15 to -20 degrees) are used as decorative dividers between headers or split hero photography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Full pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), background `#E61B23`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold `label-lg`, with `14px 28px` padding. Includes an optional forward arrow icon (`→`) on the trailing edge. Hover: scales up to `1.02` with an ambient glow (`rgba(230, 27, 35, 0.28)`).
- **Secondary CTA**: Pill shape, solid deep carbon `#111116`, white text. Used for secondary tier actions or navigational utility (e.g., Club Selector).
- **Outlined / White CTAs**: Solid white fill with red border and red label when overlaid on image hero modules.

### Membership & Plan Cards
- **Card Shell**: White container with `16px` border-radius and structured 2-part division:
  - **Header Ribbon**: Solid `#E61B23` or `#111116` band displaying the plan title (e.g., "Freedom", "Stay Fit 12 Months") with centered bold white text.
  - **Body Block**: Crisp white background, featuring the pricing display (`price-display` token) in `#E61B23`, followed by bulleted benefit rows with red or green checkmark icons.
- **Featured Tier**: Elevated slightly with a top badge: "MOST POPULAR" or trophy emoji tag.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Status Badges**: Capsule badges (`border-radius: 9999px`) using `padding: 4px 12px`.
- **Urgency Tags**: `#E61B23` fill with uppercase white text (e.g., "LIMITED TIME", "FREE ACCESS").
- **Club Amenity Badges**: Light slate background (`#EDEDF2`), carbon text, `label-sm` sizing.

### Input Fields & Forms
- **Field Styling**: Clean, high-legibility minimalist form elements. Background `#FFFFFF`, bottom border `2px solid #E0E0E6` or full outline `1px solid #D7D7DF` with `8px` border-radius.
- **Focus State**: `2px solid #E61B23` outline with immediate transition; placeholder shifts to active floating label.
- **Labels**: Outfit `label-md` with obligatory red asterisk (`*`) for required parameters.

### Form Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes**: Square `18px x 18px` with `4px` corner radius. Unchecked: `2px solid #BCBCC6`. Checked: solid `#E61B23` fill with crisp white checkmark vector.
- **Legal Terms Copy**: `body-sm` neutral copy with active links colored in `#E61B23` and underlined.

### Floating Floating "Back To Top" & Quick-Join Trigger
- **Floating Button**: Positioned fixed at bottom-right (`bottom: 24px, right: 24px`), circular `52px x 52px`, `#E61B23` fill, white upward arrow, stacked with "TOP" in `label-sm`.
